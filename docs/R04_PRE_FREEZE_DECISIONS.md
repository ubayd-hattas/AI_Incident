### Rules worth approving as guardrails immediately

- No future title list, page-family/criticality labels, final-state descriptors, future copies, or relation backfills in historical observer responses.
- Shared feed and shared metadata charges for P/PD/PCD/E; no private sensor or previous-revision retrieval for E. F is explicitly separate.
- Successful deletions and no-body edits are not ordinary GET failures; unknown never means empty text. Evidence of a request or a success sentence does not establish another action's success.
- No policy-result-driven changes to horizon, interval, q, caps, criticality, copy grouping, benchmark eligibility or support bundles. Necessary later amendments retain old settings/results, rationale, date and who had seen results.
- Preserve nulls, PCD strong control, complete parameter/phase grid, requests-and-storage fairness, archive sensitivity, and same evidence denominator.

These are research constraints, not approval of unilateral fixture states. See [SAFE TIMELINE RULES](../audit/JASWIN_V02_RECONSTRUCTION.md#safe-timeline-rules) for SAFE / modeling-only / UNSAFE / UNRESOLVED classifications.

Severity is not a prediction of the result.
