## Five tracks (page wording, consistent with docs/Hackathon_Track_Analysis_Context.md)

1. Containment — what standard would have stopped this (escape via the one permitted network egress).
2. What happened, and what breaks next — reconstruct what the incident does/doesn't establish; resolvable questions, checks somebody could run tomorrow, causal explanations that predict something.
3. Regulatory Response — draft documents a regulator/legislator could use with light edits; EU AI Office enforcement powers since 2 Aug 2026, fines up to 3% worldwide turnover; CeSIA can transmit qualifying outputs.
4. Communication: making the warning shot count — judged on grounding in the record and evidence of reach (playtest, journalist read, creator reply).
5. Open track — artifact + stated limits + a month-of-follow-up plan.
